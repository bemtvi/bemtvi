A window that opens the way you left it.

Move this window, resize it, maximize it if you like, then quit with :qa and
start bemtvi-gui again: it opens where you left it, as large, maximized or not.

:set guiposition=centered  centres it now, and every time from now on.
:set guisize=60%x70%       sizes it to a share of the screen, now and from now on.
:set guimaximize=always    maximizes it now, and every time from now on.
:set guisize&              back to remembering (the same & for the others).
