Custom tabline playground
=========================

This buffer is open in tab 1. The line across the very top of the screen is the
custom 'tabline' defined in init.lua — rendered by the same %-format engine the
statusline uses.

Open a second tab to see it list both:

    :tabedit examples/tabs/sample.txt

Now the tabline shows two labels, e.g.

    1:sample.txt(exa/tab)   2:sample.txt(exa/tab)              close

The active tab's label is drawn in the TabLineSel highlight; switch between them
with gt and gT and watch the colour follow. Each label is produced by
my_tab_label(n) in lua/myutils.lua, which:

  * lists the tab's buffers with vim.fn.tabpagebuflist(n),
  * skips side-panel buffers (NvimTree / undotree / diffpanel),
  * shows the file tail plus a 3-char-per-segment parent-dir hint, and
  * appends a `*` when vim.bo[bufnr].modified is true.

Edit this buffer (press i, type something, then <Esc>) and the `*` appears on
tab 1's label. Save with :w and it goes away.
