winhighlight — per-window highlight remap
=========================================

This file is open in the MAIN editor area, painted with the global `Normal`
background. To its left is a dock styled with:

    nx.dock.opt("left").winhighlight =
      "Normal:NormalSB,EndOfBuffer:SidebarEob,LineNr:SidebarLineNr"

The dock renders on the `NormalSB` background — a dimmer sidebar — while this
window keeps the global theme. Same editor, two backgrounds; the remap is scoped
to the dock's windows only.

Cross focus into the dock with  <C-w><C-w>h  and back with  <C-w><C-w>l  to watch
the background flip. `:DockToggle left` collapses and restores the styled sidebar.
