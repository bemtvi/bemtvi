nxvim mouse playground — every gesture below is server-hit-tested.

CLICK to place the cursor on any character on this line: the quick brown fox.
DOUBLE-CLICK a word like marmalade or constellation to select just that word.
TRIPLE-CLICK anywhere on this line to select the whole line at once, linewise.

DRAG from here ───────────────────────────────────────────► to there, and the
charwise Visual selection follows the pointer; release and it stays put for y/d.

SHIFT+CLICK extends the current selection to the click without losing the anchor.
Start a selection, then shift-click further along to grow it in place.

This very long line exists so the horizontal wheel has somewhere to go under nowrap: aaaaaaaaaa bbbbbbbbbb cccccccccc dddddddddd eeeeeeeeee ffffffffff gggggggggg hhhhhhhhhh iiiiiiiiii jjjjjjjjjj kkkkkkkkkk llllllllll mmmmmmmmmm — the end.

Open a vertical split with :vsplit and then spin the wheel over the OTHER half —
it scrolls without stealing focus, the way an inactive window scrolls in vim.
