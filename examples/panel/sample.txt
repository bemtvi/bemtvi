Press <leader>p to open the fruit panel below.

Inside the panel:
  j / k    move the cursor (ordinary motions)
  <CR>     choose the line under the cursor, then dismiss
  q / <Esc>  dismiss without choosing

The panel grabs focus while open — <C-w>w can't leave it.

The built-in listings use the same overlay. Try:
  :messages
  :ls
  :registers
  :marks
…and dismiss each with q.
