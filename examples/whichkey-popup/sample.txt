mini-which-key demo
===================

Press <Space> (the leader key) in normal mode.

A small popup opens at the cursor listing the leader menu:

    f   find file
    g   git status
    q   quit

Press one of f / g / q to pick an action — its name prints on the
message line — or press <Esc> to dismiss the popup. Either way the
popup's window and scratch buffer are torn down immediately, leaving
this buffer exactly as it was.

The popup is a real floating window over a real scratch buffer, themed
with nvim_get_hl and laid out with strdisplaywidth — the same surface
which-key.nvim drives.
