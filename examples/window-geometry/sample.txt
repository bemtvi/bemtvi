unified window geometry — viewport-relative size · alignment · margin

One vocabulary, four surfaces:

  • floats / nx.view   — width/height as cells or "50vw"/"30vh"/"50%",
                         align = "top-right"/"center"/…, margin = a gap
  • pickers            — same align + margin (no longer centered-only)
  • the bottom panel   — fractional height + a margin from the edges

Press the leader maps below (leader is the space bar):

  <leader>gf   a 40vw × 40vh float, top-right, 2-cell margin
  <leader>gc   a 60vw × 50vh float, centered
  <leader>gp   a "colours" picker, bottom-left, 3-cell margin
  <leader>gn   a 30vh bottom panel with a 1-cell edge gap

Resize your terminal while a fractional float is open: it reflows.
