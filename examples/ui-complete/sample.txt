nx.complete buffer-source playground
=====================================

The buffer source completes from words already present in THIS buffer. To see it
work, enter insert mode (press `i`) and start retyping one of the longer
identifiers below — once two characters match, a popup of candidates appears.

  configuration
  connection
  completion
  concatenate
  consequently

  parameter
  parameterize
  partition
  participant

Try, for example, typing `con` on a blank line: the popup offers `configuration`,
`connection`, `completion`, `concatenate`, and `consequently`. Press <C-n>/<Tab>
to move down the list, <C-y> (or <CR>, mapped in init.lua) to accept, or <C-e> to
dismiss and keep what you typed.

Because the source is just a scan of the buffer, the more prose you write here the
richer the completion gets — every word you have already typed becomes a
candidate for the next one.
