https://bemtvi.dev/docs    <- put the cursor on this URL and press \O to open it

btv.ui.open playground — try the maps from init.lua:

  \o   open https://bemtvi.dev in your browser (btv.ui.open + :next on the result)
  \O   open the path/URL under the cursor (gx-style, via vim.fn.expand("<cWORD>"))
  \v   open https://github.com through the vim.ui.open alias

The opener is a separate process (open / explorer / xdg-open), so nothing opens
inside the editor — watch your browser / file manager instead.
