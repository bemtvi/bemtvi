Window-local options demo
=========================

`number` and `relativenumber` are *window-local*: two splits onto this
very buffer can show different line-number gutters at the same time.

Split this window (<C-w>v or <C-w>s) and run :GutterDemo, or toggle the
gutter by hand with :setlocal number! / :setlocal relativenumber! — only
the focused split changes; its sibling keeps whatever gutter it had.
