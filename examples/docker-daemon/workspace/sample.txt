This file lives INSIDE the container, at /work/sample.txt.

When you connect to the daemon and open it, every filesystem op — the read that
filled this buffer, and the write when you :w — happens on the container, not on
your machine. The keystroke path stays local; only fs/process/watch/LSP cross the
wire.

Checklist — connect.sh passes --remote-config by default, so run these and compare
against a plain local launch:

  :WhoAmI          -> "DAEMON config ... (tabstop=8)"   (local config says LOCAL, tabstop=2)
  :set tabstop?    -> 8                                  (local config sets 2)
  :pwd             -> /work                              (the daemon's cwd, in the container)
  :r !hostname     -> the CONTAINER's hostname           (process leg runs on the daemon)

The two init.lua files are different on purpose: with --remote-config a daemon session
runs the DAEMON's config, fetched over the wire, not the client's. Connect with
REMOTE_CONFIG=0 and :WhoAmI says LOCAL instead — your config, the daemon's filesystem.
