nx.ui.select — the floating selectable-list widget
===================================================

Put the cursor anywhere and try the leader maps (leader is "\"):

    \p   pick a fruit          (plain string list)
    \c   choose a command      (format_item + act on the choice)
    \n   jump near a line       (a long, scrolling list)

In the open menu:
    j / k        move the highlight (also <C-n>/<C-p>, arrows)
    gg / G       first / last
    <CR>         confirm  ->  the choice is echoed on the message line
    <Esc> or q   cancel   ->  the cancel branch fires (on_choice(nil))

The menu floats under the cursor and grabs every key while open — the
buffer underneath is untouched until you confirm or cancel.
