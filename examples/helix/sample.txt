Selections are the noun. A motion re-selects; a verb acts on the selection now.
Try `w` to select a word, then `d` to delete it — no motion wait in between.

Multi-selection is the default, not a mode:
  - `%` selects the whole file
  - `s` spawns one selection per regex match inside the selection
  - `C` copies the selection onto the next line
Then a single verb (`d`, `c`, `~`) acts on every selection at once.
