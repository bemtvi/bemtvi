telescope.nvim on nxvim
=======================

Press Space then f then b to open the static fuzzy-finder demo — it needs no
external tools. The prompt opens in insert mode; just start typing to filter.

  <Space>fb   fuzzy-find over a built-in list of fruits   (no deps)
  <Space>ff   find files in this directory                (needs fd / rg / find)
  <Space>fg   live grep across this directory             (needs rg)

Inside the picker:
  type        filter as you go
  <C-n>/<C-p> move the selection down / up
  <CR>        accept the selection
  <Esc>       close the picker

This buffer is just here to give the editor something to open. Try <Space>ff and
fuzzy-type "init" to jump to the example's init.lua, or <Space>fg and search for a
word you can see on this screen.
