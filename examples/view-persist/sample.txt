This is the main editor buffer.

The "Notes" sidebar on the left is a plugin-owned btv.view that PERSISTS across
restarts. Add a few notes with <leader>na, then quit with :qa and re-run:

    BEMTVI_CONFIG=examples/view-persist \
      cargo run -p bemtvi -- --workspace examples/view-persist

The sidebar comes back with your notes — the editor reserved its slot, and the
plugin rebuilt the content from its own btv.shada.plugin() store. See init.lua.
