nx.view sample buffer
=====================

This file is open in the MAIN editor area. The left sidebar is a `nx.view` —
a plugin-owned, read-only content surface mounted in a dock.

- Press <leader>e to toggle the sidebar.
- Move with j / k inside it.
- Press <CR> on an entry to open it here, in the main area.

The sidebar can't be edited (try `i` or `dd` in it — nothing happens): a view is
inert to the editing grammar, so the plugin's content is never corrupted.
