gutter options demo
====================

Look left: the number column is 8 cells wide (numberwidth = 8), and a
2-column sign space (signcolumn = yes:2) sits to its left, reserved even
with no language server attached.

Press <leader>n to cycle the number-column minimum width (4 / 6 / 8).
Press <leader>s to cycle the sign column (no / auto / yes / yes:2 / auto:1-3).

These are window-local. Open a vertical split with <C-w>v, then run
    :setlocal nuw=4 scl=no
in the new window and watch only that side change.
