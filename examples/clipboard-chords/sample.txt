Ctrl+C and Ctrl+V, on the system clipboard.

1. Charwise: put the cursor on `copy me` below, press v e e <C-c>, then go
   somewhere else and press <C-v>.

   copy me please
   paste target ->

2. Linewise: press V j <C-c> on these two lines, then <C-v> anywhere.

   first line of the pair
   second line of the pair

3. Insert mode: put the cursor here, press A, then space <C-v>, then keep typing.

   append after me:

4. Command line: copy the path below charwise with 0 v $ <C-c>, then type
   `:e ` and press <C-v> — the path lands in the command line (<Esc> to bail).

examples/clipboard-chords/sample.txt

5. Round trip: copy this line with V <C-c>, then paste it into another
   application — it is on the real clipboard, not just a register.

   #!/usr/bin/env bash
