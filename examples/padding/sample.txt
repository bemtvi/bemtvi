padding demo — breathing room around the text body
===================================================

'padding' is nxvim's own window-local option (no vim equivalent). It leaves a
per-side blank margin, in screen cells, around a window's whole content box:
the number gutter, the text, and the status line all sit inside the margin.

It is window-local, so two splits onto the same file can use different margins.
The margin is server-side: the editor shrinks the text area, so wrapping,
horizontal scroll, the cursor, and mouse clicks all already account for it —
every front end (TUI, GUI, web) renders the same inset with no special-casing.

Try the steps at the bottom of init.lua. Resize the window and watch the text
keep its margin from the edges.
