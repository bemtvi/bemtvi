This is a single very long line, written as one paragraph with no newlines in the middle, so that with `:set wrap` it folds onto as many continuation rows as it needs while staying one buffer line — press j or k and the cursor moves over the whole paragraph as one line, while gj and gk step one display row at a time within it.
